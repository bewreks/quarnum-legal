Privacy Policy

Quarnum game · Effective 3 November 2026

This Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Quarnum game (the “Game”) and how you can control it.

1. Operator

The Game is operated by individual entrepreneur Dmitry Andreevich Sokolovsky, OGRNIP 323774600839507 (the “Operator”). Contact: bewreks_oplata@mail.ru.

2. Information processed

The Game does not require an account, phone number, email address, location or access to contacts.

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • game settings: sound, music, language and selected skin;
  • game progress: scores, maximum tile, booster counts and daily streak;
  • replays: seed, move sequence, game events, device model, OS version and screen size;
  • a local event log used for diagnostics, which is not sent to a server automatically.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage. The Operator has no remote access to it.

The GamePush service is used for authentication, player profile, cloud saves, leaderboards and analytics. It may process the platform player ID, display name, avatar URL, technical device and browser data, game events, and purchase or advertising data if those features are enabled. Processing is governed by GamePush: GamePush Player Privacy Policy.

3. What the Operator does not collect

The Operator does not run its own server and does not directly collect:

  • your name, phone number, email or other contact details;
  • precise location;
  • contacts, photos, files or messages;
  • advertising identifiers or ad-network data;
  • payment details.

4. Sharing

The Operator does not share local data with third parties. Authentication, profile, saves, leaderboards and analytics data are processed by GamePush under its policy. If you tap “Share” and choose an app (email, messenger or cloud storage), the replay file is sent to the recipient you select. This sharing is initiated by you.

The app store through which you installed the Game may process data under its own policy.

5. Legal basis

Local processing is necessary to run the Game and save progress. Replay sharing is initiated by you. If advertising, analytics or other online services are added in the future, this Policy will be updated before they are enabled.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is stored on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data using the operating system. Files you export are under your control and must be deleted by you.

7. Your rights

You may:

  • delete local data by clearing the Game’s data in the device settings or uninstalling the Game;
  • choose not to use “Share”;
  • contact the Operator at bewreks_oplata@mail.ru with questions about data processing.

8. Children

The Game does not ask for age and does not collect children’s personal data. If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal data to the Operator, please contact the address above.

9. Cookies and third-party SDKs

The Game uses the third-party GamePush SDK for authentication, profile, cloud saves, leaderboards and analytics. GamePush and the launch platform (VK, Odnoklassniki, etc.) may use cookies and browser local storage under their own policies. Advertising SDKs are not enabled in the current build; this Policy will be updated before they are added.
